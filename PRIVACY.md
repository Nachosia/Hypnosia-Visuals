# Privacy Policy — Hypnosia Visuals

## Short Version

Hypnosia Visuals collects only the minimum data necessary for license validation, cloud config sync, and profile features.

### What We Collect

- **Account Key & HWID hash** — for license binding and anti-abuse.
- **Display name** — shown in your public profile (if you choose to set one).
- **Playtime** — aggregated daily playtime in minutes for the "Last 7 days activity" chart on your profile.
- **Cloud configs** — configs you explicitly upload to Hypnosia Cloud.

### What We Do NOT Collect

- Minecraft passwords or credentials.
- IP addresses or detailed location data.
- Telemetry, analytics, or behavior tracking outside of the features listed above.
- Any data when you are playing offline without a Hypnosia Cloud session.

### How Data Is Stored

- Account and license data are stored on the Hypnosia License Server (`api.nachosia.site`).
- Cloud configs are stored as base64-encoded payloads tied to your account key.
- Site profile data is stored on `nachosia.site`.

### Your Rights

- You can reset your HWID binding via the in-game UI (if your role allows it).
- You can delete uploaded cloud configs at any time.
- You can request full account deletion by contacting support.
